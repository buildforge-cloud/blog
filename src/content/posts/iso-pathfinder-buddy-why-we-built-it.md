At my old job I worked on ISO 27001 certification. I found the process more
confusing than anything else.

So I built [ISO 27001 Buddy](https://iso-pathfinder-buddy.lovable.app/), an
ISO 27001 guide that runs in the browser. I wanted something light, that helps
a user along the way one step at a time. I also had a reason of my own. I
hoped that if I had to build something that explains ISO 27001 to others, I
would understand it better myself.

## How fast it came up in Lovable

I built it with [Lovable](https://lovable.dev), an AI app builder. I made it
very early, when I had little experience coding with AI.

What surprised me was how fast I had something up and running. The app has a
setup wizard, a [roadmap](https://iso-pathfinder-buddy.lovable.app/roadmap)
in ten phases, evidence templates, the Annex A controls, a glossary, and the
upkeep that comes after a certificate. There is no account. What a user
enters stays in their own browser.

Nothing went wrong in the build itself.

## Approving what the AI built

Lovable wrote each part of the guide. Before I said yes to a part, I had to
know it was right. And I couldn't judge one part without knowing where it
sat among the others.

So I went through the standard's descriptions and the support documents
again and again. Each time a new part came back, I had to work out how it
connected to the rest, or I couldn't approve it.

Building it did help me understand ISO 27001. I know how the standard hangs
together better now than I did at my old job.
