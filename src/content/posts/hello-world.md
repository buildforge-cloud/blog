I build small apps end to end, mostly with AI doing the heavy lifting on the code. This is where I'm going to write that process down as it happens, not after the fact.

## What this is

Notes from actually shipping things: a budget tracker, a cycle tracker, whatever comes next, and the infrastructure underneath all of it. Real decisions, including the ones that turned out wrong on the first try.

## What this isn't

Not a highlight reel. If something breaks in production or an approach gets thrown out after a day of work, that's a post too.
