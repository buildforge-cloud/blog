## 2026-11-24: Dependabot majors in one grouped PR a month (#135)

Twelve single Dependabot PRs were open (blog#119 to #130). Most were majors
(node 26, actions/checkout 7, typescript 7, @astrojs/mdx 8...), which the
blog#116 config left ungrouped, so each came as a PR of its own. Stefan picked
one grouped PR a month (card 4807, plan decision 4812).

`.github/dependabot.yml` now gives each of its three ecosystems a `majors`
group beside the routine one, and both groups carry the same names in every
ecosystem (`minor-and-patch`, `majors`; they were `npm-minor-and-patch` and
so on). Security updates stay ungrouped and come at once. The settings are
exactly the issue's file; the file's comments keep the blog#83 astro note and
the base-image note, and the header no longer says a major comes on its own.

`tests/dependabot.test.mjs` gained three checks (a majors group per entry,
the two group names, no group with `applies-to: security-updates`), each
with a pair test for its helper, and its Dependabot branch shapes follow the
new group names. The first two failed on the old config; the security guard
and each helper were mutation-checked.

The open single PRs are not touched here: after the first monthly run the
executive closes each one a group covers.
