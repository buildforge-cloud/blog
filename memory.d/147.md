A cold read by a fresh subagent given one blog post does not converge on a
long post: each new reader re-surfaces different points. blog#147 ran three
rounds on five posts (round 1: 8 to 37 points each; round 3: 10 to 30), and
blog#140 ran four; neither reached "Verdict: none".

**Why:** a reader told "a general web developer" flags every term of art
(canonical, noindex, NAS), every missing date, and asks for more detail, and
a fresh reader each round has no memory of what was already fixed.

**How to apply:** tell the reader who the post is for, from STRATEGY.md's
Users ("a developer whose Google traffic dropped; you know impressions,
canonical, noindex"), and to skip style, cut suggestions and wished-for
detail. Fix only real gaps: a word used before it is introduced, an unclear
"it", a missing year, a jump, a contradiction. After round 3, list what is
left in the PR's Known limits instead of looping. Re-read the post yourself
after each round's edits: a fix can leave a pronoun with no target. See
[[blog-post-voice]].
