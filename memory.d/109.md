When a fresh cold reader (a subagent given only the text) cannot follow a
detail, cut the detail unless the story needs it. Explaining it adds new
nouns, and each new noun is a new question.

**Why:** blog#109's Article took 9 rounds to reach an empty list: 29, 15,
9, 13, 10, 10, 8, 6, then none. The count rose in round 4 right after round
3 answered its questions with more detail (two first-day bugs, how the setup
file names an app's parts). Cutting those passages and a whole memory-feature
item brought it down. Stefan's decision 4425 asks for the same: a journey
with pros and cons, not specifics of single deliveries.

**How to apply:** for each item, first ask whether the sentence is needed.
Keep a story only if it carries a lesson the Article draws. Give every
role a plain name once and reuse it. Pin facts a reader asks "since when?"
or "who?" about, or drop them. Paste the full texts into each new reader's
prompt; never let it read the repo.
