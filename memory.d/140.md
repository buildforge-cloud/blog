Blog posts are written and merged weeks before their `pubDatetime`, and the
facts they state as "now" drift in between. blog#140 (24 Nov 2026) found, in
three posts queued since 31 Oct: a version named in a title that never reached
the store (Comment Finder 1.9.1; the store went 1.8.1 to 1.9.2), a fix called
future work that had shipped, and a line count called current that had moved.
The `QUEUED` test pins only dates and flags, never content.

**Why:** a queued post's sources were right on the day it was written; only a
re-read of the source repo shows what changed since.

**How to apply:** in a pre-launch check of a queued post, read each present-tense
claim (version in store, "has no issue", "N lines left", "not read yet")
against the source repo as it is today, and date what stays ("when I last
checked, on <date>"). For a Chrome extension, the store version is in its
repo's `HANDOVER_LOG.md` publish notes, not in `manifest.json`. See
[[blog-post-cold-reader]].
