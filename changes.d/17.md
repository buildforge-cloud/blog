Add after the first paragraph (the one that ends "no per-subdomain DNS step
was needed."):

    ⚠️ **`nginx.conf`'s security headers never reach a page.** They are
    `add_header` at `server` level, and nginx drops those in any `location`
    with its own `add_header`. The `.html` location sets `Cache-Control`, so
    pages get no CSP and no `Referrer-Policy`; `/robots.txt` gets both
    (`curl -sI`, 2026-09-29). A header added at `server` level is not on the
    pages. Before making the CSP reach them, add what the pages need: its
    `script-src` has no `'unsafe-inline'`, and every page carries inline
    scripts (Astro's modules, giscus, two `data-astro-rerun` blocks), so the
    CSP as written would block them. Details in `docs/ENGINEERING_LOG.md`
    (blog#17).
