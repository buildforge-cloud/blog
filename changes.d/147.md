1. **"Outbound links to other buildforge.cloud projects".** The product list
   lives in `tests/products.mjs` now: `PRODUCTS` (link text and live address,
   read by the About test in `tests/posts.test.mjs` and by a new test that the
   blog-post skill's identity block links each one) and `RETIRED` (a
   product's old names and hosts). `tests/posts.test.mjs` fails when a post,
   a page, a LinkedIn draft in `docs/linkedin/` or `strategy/STRATEGY.md`
   uses a retired one, matched in any case and across a line wrap. It fails
   on a historical mention too ("it was called ..."), so a post or an
   `_Update_` line that must name an old one needs an exception in that test
   first. A renamed or moved product: its new line in `PRODUCTS`, its old
   name or host in `RETIRED`, then grep the repo as before. This replaces the two near-identical paragraphs that end that
   section (both say the About test fails on a lost or changed product link).
