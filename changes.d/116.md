**Dependabot (blog#116, Stefan's card 4454).** `.github/dependabot.yml`
updates npm, the Dockerfile's base images and the Actions once a month, each
ecosystem's minor and patch bumps in one PR, after a 7-day cooldown; a major
comes alone. Security fixes come at once, from the repo settings, not the
file, except where an `ignore` holds them back too. Two do: astro 7.2.10 and
later while `@astrojs/mdx` is on 7 (blog#83; drop it in the PR that moves mdx
to 8), and node 23 and 25, odd majors that never become LTS (from 27 on,
Node ships one major a year and each is LTS, so nothing after 25 is held).
`tests/dependabot.test.mjs` fails on a missing or extra ecosystem, an entry
off `monthly`, without its group or its cooldown, the astro ignore out of
step with mdx, node 23 or 25 offered or 27 and later held back, and any job
on an org runner (`self-hosted`, `home`, `home-light`, or a `uses:` job that
passes or calls one) that a `dependabot/` branch could start. A compose file that runs an `image:` needs
a docker-compose entry, and a database image there a major ignore.
