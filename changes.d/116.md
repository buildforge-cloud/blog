**Dependabot (blog#116, Stefan's card 4454).** `.github/dependabot.yml`
updates npm, the Dockerfile's base images and the Actions once a month, each
ecosystem's minor and patch bumps in one PR; a major comes alone. Security
fixes come at once, from the repo settings, not the file. The npm entry
ignores astro 7.2.10 and later while `@astrojs/mdx` is on 7 (blog#83): drop
that ignore in the PR that moves mdx to 8. `tests/dependabot.test.mjs` fails
on a missing or extra ecosystem, an entry off `monthly` or without its group,
the astro ignore out of step with mdx, and any self-hosted job a
`dependabot/` branch could start. A compose file that runs an `image:` needs
a docker-compose entry, and a database image there a major ignore.
