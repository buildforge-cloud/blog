For `CLAUDE.md`, "Writing a post" (blog#104):

- A post can have a LinkedIn draft, `docs/linkedin/<slug>.md`, named after the
  post's slug. It is the text as pasted: one paragraph per line. Stefan edits
  it and posts it himself; nothing in the repo posts it.
  `tests/linkedin.test.mjs` (in `npm test`) fails when a draft has no post with
  its slug, is outside 120 to 200 words (the URL and each hashtag count as
  one), has no "AI" in its first paragraph, does not end with the line
  `https://blog.buildforge.cloud/posts/<slug>/`, has more than 3 hashtags, or
  has an em dash. `tests/linkedin.mjs` holds the rules. `REQUIRED` lists the
  drafts that must exist, so an empty folder cannot pass.
- `tests/posts.test.mjs` has a list of posts that must say in their first three
  non-empty body lines that AI wrote them ("AI") and that Stefan "edited" them.
  blog#104's post is the first. A later post that opens the same way goes on
  that list.

For `CLAUDE.md`, "Building from a worktree" (blog#104):

- The executive's worker brief now forbids a link to anything outside the
  worktree, a borrowed `node_modules` included, because removing it asks for a
  permission. So the symlink this section describes is out for a dispatched
  worker. What worked instead: `rsync` the worktree, without `.git`, into the
  scratchpad, and link the main checkout's `node_modules` in that copy. For a
  build there, also set `cacheDir` and `vite.cacheDir` in the copy's
  `astro.config.ts`, or Astro's cache (default `./node_modules/.astro`) is
  written through the link into the main checkout. A queued post can get its
  past date in the copy, so the worktree never needs it restored.
