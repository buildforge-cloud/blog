Target: CLAUDE.md, "Deployment", the "Dependabot (blog#116, ...)" paragraph:
the clause "its minor and patch bumps grouped in one PR; a major comes on its
own", and the list of what `tests/dependabot.test.mjs` fails on.

Since blog#135 (Stefan 4807) each ecosystem has two groups, named the same
everywhere: `minor-and-patch` and `majors`. So a month brings at most two
version-update PRs per ecosystem, and a major never rides in the routine PR.
Security fixes are not grouped (no group sets `applies-to`) and still come one
PR each, at once. A group's branch is `dependabot/<ecosystem>/<group>-<hash>`.
`tests/dependabot.test.mjs` also fails on an entry without its majors group,
a group named other than those two, or a group with `applies-to:
security-updates`.
