The fonts come from the repo, not Google Fonts (blog#64, 2026-10-26).
`fonts.config.mjs` lists the three families with Astro's local provider; the
files are in `src/assets/fonts/`, a `.woff` and a `.ttf` per face (satori
reads the `.ttf` for the OG images), with each family's OFL licence. They are
the bytes Google served before, so the pages did not change. Before this, a
failed Google fetch stopped the deploy (2026-10-08, 2026-10-26). `ci.yml`
points fonts.google.com, fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com at
0.0.0.0 before the build, and checks with `getent` that the block took, so
a build that fetches a font fails the PR (`tests/workflows.test.mjs` pins
the step; `tests/fonts.test.mjs` pins the faces in Google's order, since
Astro sizes the fallback font from the first face). A new font: its files in `src/assets/fonts/`, a family in
`fonts.config.mjs`, a line in `tests/fonts.test.mjs`, and a `--defined` line
in `tests/css-tokens.test.mjs`.

In the shared-parts paragraph, "Astro sets the font tokens from
`astro.config.ts`" becomes "from `fonts.config.mjs`".
