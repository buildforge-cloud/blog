For `CLAUDE.md`, "Writing a post", in the "A LinkedIn Article" paragraph
(blog#109):

- An Article is read by people who know nothing of the setup (Stefan,
  decision 4424), and tells a journey with its pros and cons, not single
  deliveries (decision 4425). `articleProblems` now also fails on an in-house
  word (executive, worker, board, card, slot, hook, worktree, `Touches`,
  agent-orchestrator, agent-os, agent-memory; whole word, plural too, any
  case but `Touches`), on a day with its month ("7 July", "November 18") or
  an ISO date, and on more than 5 different numbers. `shareProblems` fails on
  an in-house word too. A month alone ("in late September") passes. Say the
  role in plain words instead ("the lead agent", "a status page on my
  phone").
- The rules cannot see what a reader cannot follow, so an Article also gets
  a cold read before merge: a fresh subagent gets only the Article and the
  share text, and lists every word, name or reference it cannot follow. Fix
  each and ask a new reader, until one lists none (blog#109's PR has the
  rounds).
