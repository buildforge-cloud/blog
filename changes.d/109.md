For `CLAUDE.md`, "Writing a post", in the "A LinkedIn Article" paragraph
(blog#109):

- An Article is read by people who know nothing of the setup (Stefan,
  decision 4424), and tells a journey with its pros and cons, not single
  deliveries (decision 4425). `articleProblems` now also fails on an in-house
  word, on a date, and on more than 5 different numbers; `shareProblems` on an
  in-house word. Both read the text as LinkedIn shows it, so a link's address
  is skipped.
- In-house words are `IN_HOUSE` in `tests/linkedin.mjs` (the issue's list,
  plus worktree and the three tool repos' names): a whole word, plural too,
  any case but `Touches`; a hyphen joins words, so "co-worker" passes. The
  test pins the issue's words against the exported list. Say the role in
  plain words instead ("the lead agent", "a page on my phone").
- A date is a day with its month, either order, an ordinal too ("7 July",
  "July 7th", "24 Sept"), or an ISO date. A month alone ("in late
  September") passes, a capitalised word that only starts like a month ("18
  Decisions") passes, and "May" before a number is not read ("May 7 agents
  run?"). Month names are read with their capital only.
- The rules cannot see what a reader cannot follow, so an Article also gets
  a cold read before merge: a fresh subagent gets only the Article and the
  share text, pasted into its prompt, and lists every word, name or reference
  it cannot follow. Fix each and ask a new reader, until one lists none
  (blog#109's PR has the rounds).
